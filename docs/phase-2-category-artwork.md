## Visual limits and scope

The collage is not seven native 1200 x 800 originals. Crops are only 450?486 pixels wide and are upscaled 2.47?2.67x. Upscaling cannot recover detail; appropriate for responsive cards, not guaranteed sharp at full-size/high-density display. Request larger individual approved originals if full-resolution detail is required. Wide panels lose peripheral background/offerings to fit 3:2; Dolotsav loses the far-left pot and most powder bowls. The central deity, offering vessel, swings, paduka throne, butter pot and food mountain remain recognizable. Existing clipping of artwork at the collage edges cannot be restored.

Visual inspection of all seven exports found no panel mixing, headings, white separators or rounded collage corners. No production UI references changed. Approval status records approval of the supplied artwork, not approval to upload or final high-resolution quality acceptance.
